2 · What to include

Kind#Incl.Defn.Style
Scan a text to list its statement kinds.

“Definition” marks the kinds whose terms are matched later in the text (rule D4): a term they introduce links them to every later statement that uses it.

How edges are inferred
D1
a \ref in a proof → edge from the referenced statement to the proved one
D2
a \ref, link or “Theorem 2.1” inside a statement → edge to that statement
D3
“Proof of Theorem X” attaches the proof to X instead of the nearest statement
D4
a term introduced in a definition (\emph, **bold**, “quoted”, “called a …”) reappears later → edge from the definition
H1
a proof belongs to the statement just before it
H2
a corollary without references → nearest preceding theorem or proposition
H3
a lemma → the next theorem or proposition within 3 statements
H4
\index{} and \emph{} terms of non-definition statements matched in later statements (LaTeX only)
